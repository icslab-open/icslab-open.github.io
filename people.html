Supervisors ​

Li Lu

Professor, Director

luli2009@uestc.edu.cn
His research focuses on embodied AI, ultra-low-power high-performance wireless systems, IoT computing systems and wireless system security. He received his bachelor’s and master’s degrees from Zhejiang University and his Ph.D. from the State Key Laboratory of Information Security, Chinese Academy of Sciences; he was a postdoctoral researcher at HKUST, has led over 10 national research projects, published over 80 papers in venues such as ASPLOS, MICRO, MobiCom and NSDI, and won the First Prize of the National S&T Progress Award.

Chao Song

Associate Professor

chaosong@uestc.edu.cn
His research focuses on computer networks, distributed computing, data mining and machine learning, with an emphasis on graph data mining, dynamic graph stream modeling and graph-driven system optimization. He received his bachelor’s degree from Zhejiang University and his master’s and Ph.D. degrees from UESTC, was a visiting scholar at Temple University, has led and participated in NSFC and Sichuan provincial research projects, published over 40 papers in venues such as AAAI, TMC, PerCom, KBS and TKDD, and holds 5 authorized patents.
