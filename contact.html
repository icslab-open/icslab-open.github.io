Prospective Students ​

The laboratory welcomes prospective master's and doctoral students interested in intelligent computing systems, IoT, wireless embedded systems, edge intelligence, and system security.

Prospective applicants may contact us through the public email address or refer to the supervisors section on the People page to reach a relevant supervisor. Please briefly describe your research interests and include a CV, transcript, and representative project, publication, or code links when applicable.

Collaboration ​

We welcome inquiries from universities, research institutes, and industry partners on research collaboration, joint projects, student co-supervision, and system prototype validation. Please contact us through the public email address with a brief description of the background, target direction, and expected format.

Address ​

No. 4 Research Building, Qingshuihe Campus, UESTC, West Hi-Tech Zone, Chengdu, Sichuan, China.

Please make an appointment by email before visiting.
